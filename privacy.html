KRYONSEC BY RAHASEC

Privacy & data

RahaSec operates this separate KryonSec beta application portal. Contact: hello@rahasec.com. Notice version: 2026-11-22.

What the portal collects

GitHub supplies your stable account ID, username and verified primary email. Your application includes your name, company or role, evaluation goal, and the version and time of your beta and privacy acknowledgements. Founder decisions and their timestamps are recorded.

Why we use it

We use this data to identify your account, review your application, display membership status, support the beta, and protect access. Applying does not subscribe you to marketing or give permission to publish a testimonial.

Authentication and hosting

GitHub handles sign-in. RahaSec does not receive your GitHub password. The sign-in flow requests profile and email access, not repository access. The GitHub access token is used during the callback and is not stored in the application database. Cloudflare Workers and D1 host this portal and its records; provider infrastructure may process request metadata under its own terms.

Cookies and temporary records

A Secure, HttpOnly session cookie signs you in for up to 24 hours. An OAuth cookie and server record protect sign-in and expire after ten minutes. The database stores session token hashes, not raw session tokens. Temporary rate-limit records use hashed keys derived from an IP address or account ID and a time window. A scheduled cleanup removes expired records.

Retention and requests

Applications, account records and review decisions remain in the beta database until removed by RahaSec. There is no automatic application-deletion schedule in this release. Email hello@rahasec.com to request access, correction or deletion; we may need to verify your identity. Before public enrollment, RahaSec will finalize the retention schedule and any required legal operator details.

What not to submit

Do not submit passwords, API keys, raw vulnerability evidence, customer data or sensitive personal information. Arrange a separate secure channel before sharing confidential findings.

This website’s interactions

The terminal demonstration is fictional and performs no scan. Octopus cursor interactions run locally and do not transmit pointer coordinates. This design includes no advertising or analytics scripts.

Enrollment stays closed until the founder completes policy review and configures GitHub sign-in. This notice describes the implemented data flow; it is not a claim of legal certification.
