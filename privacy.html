Your data, explained.

What we collect

When you sign in, we receive your provider’s stable account identifier, name, and verified email. We request basic profile information, not access to repositories or your inbox. GitHub and Google identities remain separate.

Your application includes the professional details, profile links, testing plans, experience, and optional information you choose to submit. Do not submit credentials, target IP addresses, customer records, or sensitive findings.

How we use it

RahaSec uses this information to review beta applications, communicate review feedback through your dashboard, and arrange onboarding. The founder can see your application and keep private review notes. Applications are not publicly listed.

Feedback and First 50 cards

Feedback is saved with your account and visible only to the founder. The first 50 approved beta accounts receive a permanent edition number. A card stays private until you enable sharing and choose a public display name. Its public link shows only that display name, edition number and beta membership. You can disable the link at any time. Images you download or post elsewhere cannot be recalled by disabling the link.

Storage and access

Account and application records are stored in Cloudflare D1. Session tokens are stored as hashes; browser cookies are Secure and HttpOnly. Review changes are recorded in an audit history. Our hosting and identity providers may process security and operational logs under their own policies.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. Contact hello@rahasec.com from your account email to request correction or deletion of your account and application, or to ask about retention and provider backups. Signing out does not delete your application. No marketing subscription is created by applying. Automated decision emails are not enabled; check your dashboard.
