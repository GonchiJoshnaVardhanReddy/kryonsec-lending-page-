BETA GUIDANCE · 25 NOVEMBER 2026

A beta with clear boundaries.

Apply, then wait for review

Signing in does not grant beta access. Tell us about your work, experience, and intended evaluation. The founder may approve, decline, or request changes. You can track the decision and feedback in your dashboard.

Test only with permission

Only test systems you own or have explicit permission to assess. Agree the scope before testing. Use an isolated lab where possible, protect other people’s data, and do not upload secrets or sensitive findings to the application form.

Approval and onboarding

Approval is permission to arrange participation in the beta. Contact RahaSec to agree the testing scope and installation instructions. A website approval does not install or technically unlock an existing local CLI. The previous installer and membership system have been retired.

Give useful feedback

Describe what you tried, what happened, and what would help your workflow. Agree a secure channel before sharing sensitive findings. The beta may have limitations or defects; review results before relying on them.

No payment on application

This is a free professional beta. Applying does not require a card, create a paid subscription, or automatically authorize testing any target. Access may be withdrawn, with the current status visible in your dashboard.
