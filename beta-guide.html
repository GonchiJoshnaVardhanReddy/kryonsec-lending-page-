KRYONSEC BY RAHASEC

Beta use & scope

The professional beta is for evaluating KryonSec and sharing feedback. Access is subject to review and the onboarding arrangements agreed with RahaSec.

Free means no automatic upgrade

No payment details are requested by this landing page. There is no automatic conversion to a paid plan. Confirm access dates, usage limits and any third-party model or infrastructure costs during onboarding.

Permission comes before testing

Only test systems you own or have explicit written permission to assess. Agree the targets, allowed techniques, rate limits, testing window, stop conditions and emergency contact before a run. Beta membership is not permission to test any particular system.

Keep the environment controlled

Start in an isolated lab with known test data. Do not submit passwords, API keys, customer data or sensitive findings in an application or general feedback form. Stop testing if you discover unexpected impact or reach beyond the agreed scope.

Review the evidence

This is beta software. Results can be incomplete or incorrect and need professional judgment. Access is not a security certification, guarantee of safety or substitute for an independent assessment.

Account access and software use

Use your own account. Do not share access credentials or redistribute the software. The applicable software license and the specific beta agreement supplied during onboarding govern your use; this page does not replace those documents.

Feedback and support

Share reproducible steps, expected and actual behavior, and sanitized examples. Ask hello@rahasec.com for an agreed channel before sending confidential security evidence. Testimonials and public case studies need separate permission.
